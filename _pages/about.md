Hi, welcome to my academic webpage! I am an Assistant Professor in Machine Learning and Information Systems at the School of Computer Science and Statistics at Trinity College Dublin. Before that I spent some wonderful time at the University of Oxford. I currently lead a research group in Trinity where we focus on problems in reliable, responsible and human-centered machine learning.

My research interests are in **responsible AI**, encompassing **algorithmic fairness**, **explainability** and **human-oversight**. I regularly publish in the top ML venues (NeurIPS, ICML, IJCAI, FAccT, AAAI, AIJ and AIES). I have won two best paper awards. I also enjoy working on applied problems and won a national award for the [Best Application of AI](https://twitter.com/EoinDelaney_/status/1595436264878215169) for my work on interpretable machine learning with applications in sustainable smart agriculture. I have extensive experience with deep learning and ML frameworks (PyTorch) and designing human studies to evaluate explanations of AI systems.  

Previously, I completed my postdoc at the University of Oxford where I mainly worked on the technical side of the **Trustworthiness Auditing for AI** project with [Prof. Chris Russell](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RM2sHhYAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao), [Prof. Brent Mittelstadt](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=tP685zYAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) and [Prof. Sandra Wachter](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ZXBJVqYAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao). We built [OxonFair](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.13710) which is an open-source toolkit for measuring and enforcing fairness for high-capacity models in computer vision and NLP and we published this in NeurIPS (Main Track). Subsequent work for text-image models led by my collaborator Zihao Fu was accepted to NeurIPS the following year. 

I obtained my Ph.D. from University College Dublin. I worked at the [Insight Centre for Data Analytics](https://www.insight-centre.org/) and the VistaMilk SFI research centre. My advisors were [Dr. Derek Greene](http://www.derekgreene.com/) and [Prof. Mark T. Keane](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=bBozfc4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works). I also spent some time at Accenture Labs collaborating with [Christophe Gueret](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cgueret/?originalSubdomain=ie) on explainable AI projects. My thesis focused on counterfactual explanations with applications in time series and computer vision. My thesis was examined by [Prof. Eamonn Keogh](https://www.cs.ucr.edu/~eamonn/). 

I also have some capacity for consults that align with my research in machine learning. Please reach out if this is of interest. This could be advice on solving a particular problem, or help deciding who to hire. A recent version of my CV can be found [here](http://e-delaney.github.io/files/cv_eoin_delaney.pdf). If you have project ideas that align with my research or would like to write a proposal for funding (e.g., Marie Curie), please reach out!
